# Emberdeep economy report

Generated by `npm run economy` from the same code the game runs. Every number is a simulation with bots, not a measurement of players.
The bots see only what the lantern lights ("fog" bots) and rarely die, so treat the returns as an upper bound for real players.
Descent cost = one entry key (50 RF) + lantern flasks (20 RF each). One-off purchases (Delver mints, weapons, armor, passes) are extra spend that also goes through the 25/8/67 split; they are not in the return columns.
The pool is always 67% of everything spent. A delver gets `pool × their gold / all gold`, so the total paid out is fixed: nothing here can print or drain tokens.

## B. Sensitivity to the crowd's skill (the demo assumes 1.4 gold per RF)

One delver making 10 descents against a crowd that spent 20,000 RF and banked the given gold per RF. Cells are RF received / RF spent on the descents.

| Strategy | crowd 0.7 | crowd 1 | crowd 1.4 | crowd 2 | crowd 2.8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unperked, 1 flask | 85% | 61% | 44% | 31% | 22% |
| Unperked, 2 flasks | 171% | 124% | 91% | 65% | 47% |
| Unperked, 3 flasks | 209% | 154% | 114% | 82% | 60% |
| Friend's Blessing, 2 flasks | 199% | 145% | 106% | 76% | 55% |
| Strongest build, 3 flasks | 404% | 312% | 239% | 177% | 132% |

A cell scales with `your gold per RF / the crowd's gold per RF`. If the demo's 1.4 is wrong by a factor of two, every return above moves by about the same factor, but the sum paid out to the whole crowd stays 67% of spend.

In a tiny crowd one delver is a large part of the gold, so its own spend dilutes its share. Returns settle as the crowd grows.

## D. Whole rounds: what happens when more players run the strongest build

Each row is a whole round with players in the given proportions (share of all descents). "Paid out / spent" is everything paid to everyone divided by everything spent.

| Mix (unperked / Friend's Blessing / strongest) | Paid out / spent | Unperked | Blessing | Strongest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 100 / 0 / 0 | 67.0% | 67% | n/a | n/a |
| 90 / 8 / 2 | 67.0% | 63% | 75% | 195% |
| 70 / 20 / 10 | 67.0% | 52% | 62% | 160% |
| 50 / 30 / 20 | 67.0% | 44% | 52% | 133% |
| 20 / 30 / 50 | 67.0% | 31% | 37% | 94% |
| 0 / 0 / 100 | 67.0% | n/a | n/a | 67% |

The strongest build earns well above average only while few others play it. Its edge is paid by everyone else's gold, and it shrinks as it spreads (compare the rows); if everyone has it, the round pays every delver exactly 67% again.
That is why an edge is worth buying but cannot be farmed without limit: the more delvers buy it, the less each one's gold share is worth.
